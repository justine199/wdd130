Nigeria is a vibrant and diverse country located in West Africa, known for
its rich cultural heritage, natural resources, and entrepreneurial spirit. It is Africa's most populous nation,
home to over 200 million people and more than 250 ethnic groups, each with unique languages and traditions. Nigeria is also
a major economic hub, driven by industries such as oil, agriculture, technology, and entertainment—especially Nollywood

African continent.
